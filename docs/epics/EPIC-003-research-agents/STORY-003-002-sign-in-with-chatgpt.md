# STORY-003-002 — Sign in with ChatGPT

## Description

Implement the Sign in with ChatGPT connection on the adapter contract and credential store of STORY-003-001: registration, browser sign-in with its wait and "Cancel sign-in", the refusal of plan use, automatic renewal, revocation and sign-out, with their errors, "Sign in with another account", which replaces the one stored account only after the new sign-in validates, the remembered account email that lets a disconnected row offer "Sign in as {email}", and the `subscription` rows of Settings › Connections with "Test connection". Main holds the session and the registration and handles every connection operation; the data service only receives the connection's state and model list. Sending requests is STORY-003-003.
