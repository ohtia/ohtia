## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Accepting creates Josefa's task with exactly the request and project data, without the conversation, under version 2's instructions and an input that starts with the consultation line.
- [ ] The card names Josefa with the display name of the connection that will receive her request; after acceptance it keeps the recorded connection when her preference changes; the request renders as Markdown and the reason as plain text.
- [ ] Accepting or declining a consultation that is no longer `proposed` is rejected with `proposal_not_waiting`, changes nothing, and the card shows its current state.
- [ ] The open card shows "Waiting for your answer" below its title and each decided state its sentence and icon there; a card right below Virgilio's message shows no `VoiceLabel` of his.
- [ ] Declining or replacing a proposal sends nothing, and a replaced proposal cannot be accepted.
- [ ] A failed "Ask Josefa" or "Decline consultation" keeps the card open with its notice and repeats with the same operation identifier.
- [ ] Invalid proposals record their reason, show their notice and store no contribution.
- [ ] Virgilio's next input carries Josefa's plan in the user role with the "Message from Josefa, the reference librarian:" line.
- [ ] Each consultation proposal state and invalid reason gives Virgilio's next input its exact note at its place, and a note whose place falls among omitted messages is not sent.
- [ ] "Try again" of a failed consultation repeats the same request without the conversation's messages, and a consultation that failed with `not_eligible` offers no "Try again".
- [ ] A message written while Josefa works is answered by Virgilio after her, seeing her plan, and its waiting notice names Josefa; when her result is saved while his reply task waits, that task is replaced and its input ends with her plan.
- [ ] Accepting after Josefa's connection changed is rejected with `destination_changed`, saves nothing and shows its notice; accepting, "Try again" or "Start reply again" while another consultation task is unfinished is rejected with `consultation_unfinished` and shows its notice.
- [ ] "Start reply again" of an uncertain consultation asks with the double-charge confirmation and records the accepted warning, and only the consultation's latest task offers "Try again" or "Start reply again".
- [ ] With inference concurrency set to one, the consultation, Virgilio's next reply and Stop work, in order.
- [ ] The card and the invalid-proposal notice are timeline items of rank 2 whose revision is the intervention's `revision`, an invalid proposal replaces no open one, and a consultation task's notice sits below the card before the task starts.
- [ ] `docs/agents/agent-virgilio.md` places a single consultation under "Propose a next research action" and plans of two or more contributions under "Plan bounded collaboration".
- [ ] With the researcher's account, Virgilio proposes a consultation, Josefa's plan appears after acceptance, states its assumptions and lists its open points without asking questions, and Virgilio answers a question about it.
