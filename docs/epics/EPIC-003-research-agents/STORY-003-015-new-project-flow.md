## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All five ways to start run `project.new` and create one project, a double click creates only one, and "New project" is the first item of File.
- [ ] With no connection in the state `connected`, including one that is `expired` or `connecting`, nothing is created and the dialog appears, naming no service.
- [ ] The `docs/design-system.md` Button rule lists “Try again” wherever an action is repeated among its exceptions, and the `circle-x` row also names declined and replaced proposals.
- [ ] Every `ActionProposalCard` state and invalid-proposal notice shows its exact text, and a card takes the place of a reply that carried only the proposal.
- [ ] The open card shows "Waiting for your answer" with `circle-dashed` right below its title, each decided state shows its sentence and icon there, and neither the card nor an invalid-proposal notice right below Virgilio's message repeats his `VoiceLabel`; the empty setup conversation shows Ohtia's text as a centered secondary paragraph.
- [ ] "Confirm setup" is disabled while Virgilio replies, and confirming twice gives the same result; "Correct setup" and "Reject setup" work, and the composer's placeholder returns to "Message Virgilio" after sending.
- [ ] A failed creation shows its dialog and changes nothing, and each failure of "Confirm setup" and "Reject setup" shows the notice of its error code, a proposal decided meanwhile in another window shows its state without a notice, and an unconfirmed result shows its notice.
- [ ] A message rejected by the pending limit or with `input_too_large` stays in the composer with its notice.
- [ ] With the researcher's account, setups in Spanish for Argentina and in English complete and a rejected proposal is followed by a new one.
