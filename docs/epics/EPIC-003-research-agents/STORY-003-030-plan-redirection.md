## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Changes before and after a contribution starts behave as in scenario 19.
- [ ] A completed contribution cannot change, and a running one stops before its replacement starts.
- [ ] A change beyond the plan's agents or limits is rejected, and a new plan links to the previous one, whatever outcome ended it, with its own limits while the earlier plan's usage stays recorded.
- [ ] "Apply change" past the event cap shows its notice and keeps the proposal open, and `docs/agents/agent-collaboration.md` lets a redirection alter work that has not completed.
- [ ] Failed, uncertain and blocked contributions can be removed or replaced.
- [ ] A plan whose required contribution was removed, or replaced by one that then completes, becomes `complete` and creates its continuation once every required contribution of the current graph is completed and nothing in it is waiting or active; the plan card's counts leave out removed and superseded contributions, and the synthesis still discloses them.
- [ ] Each invalid reason is stored and shown, a request over 8 KiB is rejected, a repeated key is rejected with `plan_key_duplicate`, and stored cancelled contributions get their cancellation reason.
- [ ] `plan.cancel`, `plan.finish` at the event cap, the composer's Stop of a consultation and "Cancel work and quit" set `cancellation_reason` on every contribution they cancel, and no cancelled contribution lacks one.
- [ ] Each replacement on the change card shows its new request, reason, "Required" or "Optional" and prerequisite keys, and marks with "(changed)" exactly the values that differ from the contribution it replaces, "After: none (changed)" included.
- [ ] The tool definition is sent exactly as in SPEC-003-035, and every kind of change, key and change count is decoded as stated.
- [ ] Applying adds the new contributions at the new plan version with their shares, starts those whose prerequisites are complete, keeps earlier purposes and shared contexts, and later activations receive the shared context in force.
- [ ] A `planChange.apply` whose recheck fails is rejected with `plan_change_invalid` and its `reason`, saves nothing and shows its notice.
- [ ] Each proposal state gives Virgilio's next input its note, and from version 5 the plan-status note names each contribution's requirement and prerequisites.
- [ ] `contribution_synthesis` version 2 receives removed and replaced contributions in their one-line layouts, under its added instructions, and "Based on:" names them.
- [ ] A failed "Apply change" or "Decline change" keeps the card open with its notice and repeats with the same operation identifier, and the card and the invalid notice have rank 2 and move from revision 1 to 2 when the proposal is applied, declined or replaced.
- [ ] With the researcher's account, removing and changing contributions of a running plan ends in a correct synthesis.
