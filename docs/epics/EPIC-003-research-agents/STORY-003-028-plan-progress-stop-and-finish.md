## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Right after "Run plan" the state label reads "Running · 0 of {total} done" in place of "You ran this plan."; "Waiting for {keys}" names only unfinished prerequisites; "Show request" reveals "Request:" and "Reason:"; every state label carries its icon; "Stop plan" and "Finish plan" are secondary.
- [ ] Every progress state is shown and the confirmations work with Esc.
- [ ] A contribution refused at activation shows its "Not sent" text, and blocked, paused and stopping contribution rows and every synthesis row state show their texts and actions.
- [ ] A failed "Stop plan" or "Finish plan" and a "Try again" rejected with `limit_reached`, `destination_changed`, `input_changed`, `plan_unfinished` or `event_limit_reached` show their notices; the `plan_unfinished` notice of "Run plan", "Ask Josefa" and a synthesis retry reads "Another plan in this conversation hasn't finished. - [ ] The request counter "Used {n} of up to {max_requests} requests." matches the requests dispatched.
- [ ] `plan_state_changed` for "Finish plan" changes nothing and rereads the card without a notice.
- [ ] "Stop plan" shows while the plan is unfinished, including while only the synthesis runs, with the matching dialog body, and "Finish plan" only in "Needs your attention"; rows of an ended plan offer no contribution "Try again" while the synthesis row keeps its own; a stopped synthesis, and one whose continuation "Cancel work and quit" withdrew, read "Stopped" after Ohtia opens again; a synthesis whose continuation a crash holds reads "Paused: Ohtia stopped before it started" with "Continue all ({n})".

- [ ] `docs/design-system.md` maps the agent-work state icons and states that a state label never reuses an icon mapped to another meaning.
